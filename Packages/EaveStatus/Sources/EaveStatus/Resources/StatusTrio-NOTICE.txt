Modified for Eave menu-bar integration (2026-10-12).
Eave owns application lifecycle, settings window, battery readings and audio writes.
This module embeds the status icon and panel without Status Trio's application,
updater or telemetry. Optional Dock, direct DDC audio and a bundled mobile-device
helper are integrated with Eave's lifecycle and volume control owner.
